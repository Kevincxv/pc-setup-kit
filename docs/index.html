Questions

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Free and open source. No account - you pick a local username during a fresh install, and the optional AI assistant uses your own Claude account only if you switch it on.

What exactly does it change?

It removes Windows bloat and ads (Copilot, Widgets, Teams, OneDrive, ~50 preinstalled apps), turns off telemetry and the built-in AI features, applies gaming tweaks and installs Steam, Discord, Chrome and the NVIDIA App on NVIDIA cards. The app's Optimizations tab lists every change, with a switch for each.

How does it update?

By itself, in the background - your PC gets each new version at its next login, only after that version passed a full test install. Nothing to do.

Installing Windows from scratch?

A USB stick (8 GB or bigger) that installs a slimmed-down Windows 11 and sets the PC up by itself - no Edge, Teams, Outlook, OneDrive or Xbox app, and Windows can still update and repair itself.

  1. On any Windows PC, open the file below and pick the stick (it's erased). It downloads Windows 11 from Microsoft: 20-40 minutes.
  2. Boot the new PC from the stick (often F8, F11 or F12) and pick the drive to install on - careful: delete partitions only on the drive you want wiped.
  3. Pick a username and password. A "Setting up this PC" window shows the progress - keep the stick in until it's done.

How do I remove it?

In the app: Settings > Tools > Uninstall Messiah. Or run C:\PCSetupKit\uninstall.ps1 (add -RevertTweaks to put Windows' settings back). Your apps, files and games stay.
